How it works

1. Get a key

After you buy, a licence key arrives by email. It starts with RP-.

2. Paste it

In the Rowpack sidebar open "Licence key", paste the key and click Activate.

3. No limit

The key runs for a month or a year from the day you activate it. The sidebar shows your plan and its end date.

Questions

Does Pro send my data anywhere? No. The key is checked inside the add-on, which has no server. Your sheets and documents stay in your Google account.

One key, how many people? A key is for one person's Google account. For a team, write and we will send team pricing.

How do I renew? Buy another key and activate it, even before the current one ends: its time is added to what is left.

What happens when a key ends? Rowpack goes back to the free plan, 10 documents a day. Nothing you made is touched.

Refunds? If Rowpack does not work for you, write within 14 days of buying and you get your money back.
