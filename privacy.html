Rowpack

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Rowpack is an add-on for Google Sheets. It creates Google Docs and PDF files from rows of a spreadsheet and a Google Docs template. This policy explains what data it uses and why.

What the add-on accesses

  • The spreadsheet you run it in: the header row and the data rows of the sheet you choose, to group them and fill the template.
  • The template you choose or that the add-on creates: to read its placeholders and copy it.
  • Files the add-on creates: the generated documents and PDFs, the templates it creates for you, and its folder "Rowpack" in your Drive.

The add-on cannot see other files in your Drive. It uses the drive.file permission, which only covers files you choose with the Google file picker and files the add-on created.

Google permissions and why they are needed

  • spreadsheets.currentonly: read the rows of the current spreadsheet and write the list of generated files to a sheet named "Rowpack results".
  • drive.file: copy the template, fill the copy with your data through the Google Docs API, create the folder, and save the PDF files. This permission covers only files you picked and files the add-on created.
  • script.container.ui: show the sidebar and dialogs in Google Sheets.

Where data is stored

Processing runs in Google Apps Script inside your Google account, and the PDF files are exported and saved by the sidebar in your browser, which talks directly to Google Drive. The add-on has no server of its own and sends no spreadsheet or document content outside Google. It stores three small items with Google's script property storage: your last settings for the spreadsheet (sheet name, group column, template link, file name pattern), the ID of its "Rowpack" folder in your Drive, and a counter of documents created today, used for the free plan limit.

The add-on does not use analytics, advertising or tracking, does not sell data, and does not use your data to train machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Rowpack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Generated files and templates are ordinary files in your Drive: delete them, or the "Rowpack" folder, like any other file. Uninstalling the add-on and removing its access in your Google Account settings (Security, Third-party apps) stops all access. Settings stored in a spreadsheet are removed when that spreadsheet is deleted.
